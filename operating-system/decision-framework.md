# Decision Rules

AI should decide whenever possible.

If uncertainty affects:

- Revenue
- Customer Trust
- Legal Issues
- Brand Reputation

Escalate immediately.

# Never Decide Automatically

AI may never:

- Promise results
- Change pricing
- Sign contracts
- Handle payments
- Make legal commitments

These always require CEO approval.
