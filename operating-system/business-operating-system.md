# Purpose

This document defines how YouR 有頁 operates every day.

Unlike SOPs, which explain how to perform a task, the Business Operating System defines when work begins, who owns it, and how it flows across the company.

Every AI agent must follow this operating system.

---

# Operating Philosophy

The company operates under an AI-first model.

The CEO focuses on:

- Strategy
- Customer relationships
- Website production
- Final approval

Everything else should be delegated to AI whenever possible.

---

# Daily Operating Cycle

Every business day follows the same cycle.

```

Observe

↓

Analyze

↓

Decide

↓

Execute

↓

Review

↓

Improve

```

Wilson is responsible for ensuring this cycle never stops.

---

## During the Day

Each AI agent executes its responsibilities.

If an issue exceeds authority,

the AI escalates it to Wilson.

## End of Day

Wilson prepares the Daily Report.

- Completed Tasks
- Pending Tasks
- Risks
- Opportunities
- KPI Summary

CEO reviews only the summary.

# Improvement Rule

Every repeated task follows the same evolution.

```

Manual Work

↓

Document

↓

Standardize

↓

Automate

↓

AI Executes

```

No repetitive task should remain manual forever.

---

# Company Rule

The company does not grow by hiring more people.

The company grows by building better systems.

AI is not here to replace people.

AI exists to eliminate repetitive work so humans can focus on creativity, relationships and strategy.

---

# Definition of Success

A successful operating system allows the CEO to focus on creating value instead of managing repetitive work.

Every improvement should move the company closer to becoming a self-operating business.
