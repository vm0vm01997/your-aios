# Project Status

> Standard Project Status Definitions

---

# Purpose

This document defines every project status used inside YouR AIOS.

All AI agents must use the same status names.

---

# Pipeline

Lead

Potential customer identified.

↓

Qualified

Lead meets qualification requirements.

↓

Discovery

Business interview in progress.

↓

Proposal

Quotation and proposal prepared.

↓

Negotiation

Customer reviewing proposal.

↓

Won

Customer accepted proposal.

↓

Deposit Received

Project officially starts.

↓

Planning

Research and planning.

↓

Production

Website under development.

↓

QA

Internal review.

↓

Client Review

Waiting for customer feedback.

↓

Revision

Changes in progress.

↓

Ready for Delivery

Final confirmation.

↓

Delivered

Website launched.

↓

Maintenance

Subscription or support period.

↓

Completed

Project successfully completed.

↓

Archived

Historical reference only.

---

# Project Rules

Every project must always have one and only one status.

Wilson is responsible for keeping project status updated.

---

# Status Changes

AI may update operational status.

Only the CEO may change:

- Won
- Cancelled
- Completed

These affect business records.

---

# KPI Connection

Wilson tracks:

- Time spent in each stage
- Delays
- Conversion rate
- Completion rate

This information is used for future optimization.
