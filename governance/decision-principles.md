# Decision Principles

---

Every important decision should be evaluated against the following questions.

## Customer

Will this help the customer achieve better business results?

---

## Trust

Will this increase or decrease trust?

---

## Simplicity

Does this simplify the business?

---

## Scalability

Can this process scale without significantly increasing manual work?

---

## Profitability

Does this create sustainable profit?

---

## Automation

Can repetitive work be automated?

---

## Brand

Does this strengthen the YouR 有頁 brand?

---

## Knowledge

Can this decision become reusable knowledge?

---

## Final Rule

When two options are equally good,

choose the one that:

- scales better
- creates reusable systems
- reduces manual work
