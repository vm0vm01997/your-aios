# AI Synchronization Protocol (ASP)

## Purpose

Ensure all AI systems operate from the same repository state.

---

## Rule 1

No AI may modify repository content without producing a Sync Report.

---

## Rule 2

Every completed task must include:

- Files modified
- Reason
- Business impact
- Related departments
- Related agents

---

## Rule 3

Wilson reviews every significant repository modification.

---

## Rule 4

Repository consistency takes precedence over speed.

---

## Rule 5

All AI must reference the latest Repository State before beginning new work.

---

## Rule 6

If two AI produce conflicting changes,

Wilson determines the final version.

---

## Rule 7

No AI may overwrite another AI's work without documenting the reason.
