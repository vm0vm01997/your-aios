# Security Policy

---

## Purpose

Protect company information, customer data, and digital assets.

---

## Repository

Sensitive information should never be committed to Git.

Examples:

- API Keys
- Passwords
- Access Tokens
- Private Certificates

---

## Authentication

Enable two-factor authentication whenever supported.

---

## Backups

Critical business data should be backed up regularly.

---

## Access Control

Grant the minimum permissions necessary.

Review permissions periodically.

---

## Customer Data

Only collect information necessary to provide services.

Protect customer privacy at all times.

---

## AI

AI should not expose confidential information in generated content.

---

## Incident Response

If a security incident occurs:

1. Identify the issue.
2. Limit further impact.
3. Notify the CEO.
4. Investigate the root cause.
5. Improve the process.
