# Playbooks

> Standard operating playbooks for YouR AIOS.

---

## Purpose

Playbooks define **how AI agents execute recurring business processes**.

Unlike SOPs, which explain individual tasks, playbooks coordinate multiple AI agents to achieve a business objective.

Every playbook contains:

- Trigger
- Owner
- Participants
- Workflow
- Deliverables
- KPI
- Exit Criteria

---

## Current Playbooks

- Lead Generation
- Client Discovery
- Proposal
- Website Production
- Content Production
- SEO Optimization
- Customer Follow-up
- Monthly Business Review
- Incident Response

---

## Principle

A playbook should allow an AI workflow to execute with minimal CEO intervention.

Every playbook should be continuously improved based on real business experience.
