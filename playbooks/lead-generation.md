# Lead Generation Playbook

## Objective

Generate a consistent pipeline of qualified leads.

---

## Trigger

Every weekday at 08:00.

---

## Owner

Jason

---

## Participants

Lucy

Danny

Wilson

CEO

---

## Workflow

1. Search 10 potential companies.
2. Lucy evaluates each company.
3. Score based on:
   - Website quality
   - SEO performance
   - Brand image
   - Digital maturity
4. Jason prioritizes Top 5.
5. Danny creates CRM records.
6. Wilson prepares daily lead report.
7. CEO reviews and contacts prospects.

---

## Deliverables

- 10 new prospects
- Top 5 priority leads
- CRM updated

---

## KPI

- 50 new leads per week
- Lead qualification accuracy
- Contact rate

---

## Exit Criteria

All qualified leads entered into CRM.
