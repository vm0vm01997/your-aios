# UX Principles

The objective of every website is not beauty.

The objective is business growth.

---

Every page should answer:

Who are we?

↓

Why should customers trust us?

↓

What problem do we solve?

↓

Why choose us?

↓

What should visitors do next?

---

UX Principles

- Reduce clicks
- Reduce confusion
- Increase trust
- Guide attention
- Maximize conversion

---

Every design decision should support these principles.
