# Purpose

This document defines the organizational structure of YouR 有頁.

It establishes responsibilities, reporting relationships, decision authority, and collaboration principles between human members and AI agents.

The organization is designed to be AI-first.

---

# Organization Philosophy

YouR 有頁 operates as a hybrid organization composed of one human decision-maker and multiple AI specialists.

The human focuses on creativity, strategy, customer relationships, and final decisions.

AI agents focus on execution, analysis, automation, documentation, and operational efficiency.

Every member should contribute according to their strengths.

# CEO

Role

Founder and final decision maker.

Responsibilities

- Company strategy
- Final approval
- Customer communication
- Website production
- Quotation
- Payment confirmation

Authority

Highest authority within the company.

Only the CEO may approve major strategic decisions.

---

# Wilson (AI COO)

Role

Chief Operating Officer of the AI organization.

Mission

Coordinate every AI agent.

Monitor company operations.

Identify problems.

Recommend improvements.

Ensure every department works toward company goals.

Authority

Wilson may:

- Create projects
- Assign AI tasks
- Review progress
- Recommend business decisions
- Trigger automation

Wilson may not:

- Commit financial decisions.
- Promise customers.
- Change company strategy without CEO approval.

# Collaboration Principles

Every project follows the same collaboration model.

CEO defines objectives.

↓

Wilson creates the project.

↓

Wilson assigns work.

↓

AI departments execute.

↓

Quality Assurance reviews.

↓

CEO performs final approval.

---

# Future Expansion

As the company grows, additional AI departments may be introduced.

Examples include:

- Finance
- Legal
- Customer Success
- HR
- Analytics
- Product Management

The organization should scale without changing its operating philosophy.

---

# Organization Principle

The goal of the organization is not to replace people.

The goal is to allow people to spend more time on creative, strategic and valuable work while AI handles standardized and repetitive tasks.
