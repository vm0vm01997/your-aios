# Decision History

## 2026

### Repository Strategy

**Decision**

GitHub is the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for YouR AIOS.

**Reason**

Company knowledge should exist in one authoritative location to avoid duplication between GitHub, Notion and AI prompts.

**Expected Outcome**

Consistent documentation, easier AI collaboration, and better long-term maintainability.

**Approved By**

CEO

---

### Company Positioning

**Decision**

YouR 有頁 positions itself as a Digital Growth Consulting Company rather than a Website Design Company.

**Reason**

Websites are only one tool used to help clients grow.

The company's true value lies in business strategy, digital systems, AI automation, SEO, GEO, CRM, and long-term consulting.

**Expected Outcome**

Higher perceived value.

Long-term customer relationships.

Greater differentiation.

**Approved By**

CEO

---

### Pricing Strategy

**Decision**

The company will never compete primarily through low prices.

**Reason**

Long-term trust and business value are more important than winning projects through discounts.

**Expected Outcome**

Higher-quality clients.

Better profit margins.

Sustainable growth.

**Approved By**

CEO

---

### AI-first Organization

**Decision**

The company adopts an AI-first operating model.

**Reason**

AI improves operational efficiency and allows the founder to focus on strategy, creativity, customer relationships, and final decision-making.

**Expected Outcome**

Scalable operations without increasing headcount at the same pace.

**Approved By**

CEO
