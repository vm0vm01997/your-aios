# Purpose

This roadmap defines the long-term development direction of YouR 有頁.

It aligns business priorities, AI development, and operational goals while maintaining flexibility for future opportunities.

The roadmap should be reviewed every six months.

---

# Guiding Principle

Growth should be systematic, sustainable, and measurable.

The objective is not rapid expansion at any cost, but building a company that continues to grow for decades.

---

# Stage 1｜Foundation

## Objective

Validate the business model.

Build reputation.

Acquire the first successful customers.

Generate stable cash flow.

---

## Business Goals

- Acquire the first paying clients.
- Build strong case studies.
- Establish a recognizable brand.
- Standardize service delivery.
- Build the YouR AIOS knowledge base.

---

## AI Goals

- Build the AI organization.
- Document every workflow.
- Create reusable SOPs.
- Automate repetitive work.
- Integrate GitHub, Notion and n8n.

---

# Stage 2｜Growth

## Objective

Increase recurring revenue.

Reduce operational dependence on the founder.

---

## Business Goals

- Expand website subscription services.
- Increase maintenance contracts.
- Launch SEO and GEO services.
- Improve customer retention.
- Strengthen referral channels.

---

## AI Goals

- AI-driven customer management.
- AI-assisted sales.
- AI-generated content.
- AI project management.
- AI reporting dashboard.

---

# Stage 3｜Scale

## Objective

Become a digital growth consulting company.

---

## Business Goals

- Deliver AI automation services.
- Build CRM consulting capability.
- Develop online education.
- Expand strategic partnerships.
- Strengthen market authority.

---

## AI Goals

- Self-managed AI departments.
- Cross-agent collaboration.
- Predictive business analytics.
- Automated project creation.
- Automated KPI tracking.

# Five-Year Vision

Within five years, YouR 有頁 aims to become a company that consistently attracts at least ten new customers every month.

Clients recognize the company not because it builds websites, but because it helps businesses achieve sustainable growth.

---

# Long-term Success Indicators

Success is measured by:

- Customer growth
- Client retention
- Referral rate
- Recurring revenue
- Operational efficiency
- Brand reputation
- AI maturity
