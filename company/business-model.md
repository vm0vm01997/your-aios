# Purpose

This document defines how **YouR 有頁** creates value, generates revenue, and builds long-term customer relationships.

The goal is not simply to sell websites, but to become a long-term digital growth partner for every client.

---

# Business Philosophy

Websites are not our final product.

A website is the entry point to a long-term business relationship.

Our real product is **business growth**.

Every service we provide should help clients:

- Increase visibility
- Generate more qualified leads
- Improve operational efficiency
- Strengthen brand value
- Build sustainable growth

---

# Business Growth Strategy

The company grows through three stages.

These stages describe the evolution of **YouR 有頁**, not the mandatory journey of every client.

Different clients may enter through different services.

---

# Phase 1｜Foundation

## Objective

Build cash flow.

Validate the market.

Accumulate successful case studies.

Establish brand credibility.

---

## Core Services

### One-page Sales Website

A lightweight solution for businesses that need to launch quickly.

Suitable for:

- New businesses
- Marketing campaigns
- Product launches
- Landing pages

---

### Brand Website

The company's core service.

Suitable for businesses that need:

- Future expansion

Modules may include:

- Shopping cart
- Membership system
- Blog

## Success Metrics
